# v3 primary-source citation audit

The manuscript cites every primary source family used in the current catalogue:

- Juodžbalis et al. (JADES BLAGN)
- Taylor et al. (CEERS/RUBIES)
- Matthee et al. (EIGER/FRESCO)
- Lin et al. (ASPIRE)
- Harikane et al. (NIRSpec BLAGN)
- Davis et al. (THRILS)
- Ren et al. (ALPINE–CRISTAL–JWST candidates)
- Bogdán et al. and Zou et al. (UHZ1 evidence history)
- Scholtz et al. (JADES narrow/high-ionization candidates)
- Dayal (growth-model reference)

It also cites the ancillary papers that contribute catalogue coordinates or
source context:

- Hutchison et al. (THRILS programme coordinates and redshifts)
- Goulding et al. (UHZ1 spectroscopy and host context)

The versioned JADES DR3 GOODS-S prism catalogue is identified in the manuscript
and extraction notes as the coordinate source for the Scholtz rows; its archived
SHA-256 is recorded in the provenance registry and canonical catalogue rows. DOIs or exact source versions
are given in the manuscript when available. In particular, Zou et al. is cited
as arXiv `2603.24893v1`, matching the evidence-history table.
