# v2 Figure Inventory (v1 Catalogue)

This inventory maps the v2 figure prototypes derived from the v1 catalogue to likely main-text,
appendix, and exploratory uses. The scientific posture is triage-oriented:
figures identify high-leverage objects and assumption sensitivity, not proof of
any single seed or accretion channel.

## Figure-Claim Guardrails

- Curves and ranking metrics are diagnostic assumptions, not fitted histories.
- High required `f_Edd` does not by itself prove super-Eddington growth occurred.
- High required seed mass is a triage metric, not seed-channel evidence or a
  single-formation-channel discriminator.
- Tentative high-pressure objects should be framed as measurement-sensitive
  follow-up priorities, not as secure formation-model constraints.
- Compatibility plots should distinguish undergrowth from overgrowth and should
  not be used as a synonym for physical possibility.
