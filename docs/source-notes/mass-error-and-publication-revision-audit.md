# Numerical mass and publication-version audit

Independent checks cover all 248 numerical mass measurements and both error
bounds: 744 fields. All 37 source families have anchors, with 2,079 comparisons
in total; some checks overlap. See [validation coverage](../../data/validation/README.md).

The September audit identified the Killi conversion correction below. The
October additions are included in the current coverage and results reported here.

## Killi J0647-1045

The final published abstract, deposited by the publisher with Crossref
([DOI record](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1051/0004-6361/202348857)), gives
z=4.5319 and M=8(+0.5,-0.4)e8 Msun. The old extraction stored logM=8.90 with
symmetric +/-0.03 dex. The corrected values are logM=8.90308999,
upper error=0.02632894, and lower error=0.02227639 dex, computed as
log10(M), log10((M+upper)/M), and log10(M/(M-lower)). The source record is
identified in the shared provenance registry; the independently recorded
expected values preserve the conversion check. The original preprint remains a separate coordinate-source record.
No lensing correction or additional mass scatter is introduced.

## Meaning of sampled errors

The catalogue samples reported mass errors, whose source-defined components need
not be purely statistical. ZS7's published 0.4-dex error already includes
calibration scatter. Its error is retained once; the catalogue adds no extra
scatter. `reported_mass_error_scope` explicitly identifies this case and
leaves other published components undecomposed rather than asserting that
all source posteriors have the same statistical interpretation.

Science tables use `reported_mass_errors_sampled`, `reported_mass_error_model`,
and `additional_systematic_scatter_added` to distinguish reported errors from
added scatter. The legacy field `mbh_statistical_uncertainty_kind` retains
source-defined error semantics; its name does not imply calibration effects
have been removed. Additional method envelopes remain separate.

## Baccus publication sensitivity

The [published Table 1](https://content.cld.iop.org/journals/0004-637X/1006/2/165/revision1/apjae7de7t1_mrt.txt)
is retained as `data/raw/baccus_published_table1.txt`, an input to the
sensitivity calculation, and hashed independently from the frozen arXiv v1 table. Forty-four
frozen objects match by exact ID and within 0.5 arcsec. Five have no counterpart
within 0.5 arcsec; no ambiguous identity is forced. Of the 44 matches, 32 masses
change, with shifts from -0.10 to +0.04 dex.

`src/internal/assess_baccus_revision.py` substitutes published redshifts, masses,
quoted mass errors, luminosities, and Eddington ratios for matched objects.
Two scenarios retain or omit the five unmatched frozen objects, while keeping
evidence classifications and mass-method taxonomy fixed. No new sources are
admitted. The same deterministic 10,000-draw model is used for each scenario.
The two result tables are generated by the science notebook and independently
rebuilt by `verify_versions`:

- `results/v3/tables/v3_baccus_revision_comparison.csv`: all 49 objects, identity
  status, source values, shifts, and per-object growth/uncertainty diagnostics.
- `results/v3/tables/v3_baccus_revision_summary.csv`: the frozen baseline and
  both published-value scenarios.

All three scenarios have 17 central/median requirements above unity, 13 at p16,
19 at p84, and 11 with P(f_Edd>1)>=0.95. The baseline and unmatched-retained
scenarios contain 241 numerical objects; omitting unmatched rows leaves 236.
Their top five are CAPERS-LRD-z9, UNCOVER-20466, CANUCS-LRD-z8.6, GN-z11,
and GS-20057765. Individual lower-ranked values and ranks can change.

The conservative publication baseline contains 223 primary and 238 expanded
objects. Central/p16/probability threshold counts are 11/9/8 and 17/13/11,
respectively. The
[publication comparison](../../results/v3/selected_samples/tables/publication_baccus_revision_summary.csv)
applies the evidence and identity exclusions to each substitution scenario.

These results test measurement revisions at fixed classifications and selection;
they do not admit newly listed objects or establish population fractions.
The original preprint measurements remain canonical. The
[current catalogue summary](../../results/v3/tables/v3_baccus_revision_summary.csv)
records the scenario counts and settings.
