# Numerical mass and publication-version audit

Reviewed 2026-09-04. All 244 admitted numerical mass measurements were compared
with source-table central masses and both error bounds: 732 fields. The other
243 measurements agree with their recorded source versions. Killi required a
conversion correction; this changes no membership or headline count.

## Killi J0647-1045

The final published abstract, deposited by the publisher with Crossref
([DOI record](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1051/0004-6361/202348857)), gives
z=4.5319 and M=8(+0.5,-0.4)e8 Msun. The old extraction stored logM=8.90 with
symmetric +/-0.03 dex. The corrected values are logM=8.90308999,
upper error=0.02632894, and lower error=0.02227639 dex, computed as
log10(M), log10((M+upper)/M), and log10(M/(M-lower)). The source record is
stored under `data/validation/source_inputs/` and pinned by the provenance
manifest. The original preprint remains a separate coordinate-source record.
No lensing correction or additional mass scatter is introduced.

## Meaning of sampled errors

The atlas samples reported mass errors, whose source-defined components need
not be purely statistical. ZS7's published 0.4-dex error already includes
calibration scatter. Its error is retained once; the atlas adds no extra
scatter. `reported_mass_error_scope` explicitly identifies this case and
leaves other published components undecomposed rather than asserting that
all source posteriors have the same statistical interpretation.

Regenerated science tables replace `reported_statistical_errors_sampled` with
`reported_mass_errors_sampled`, `statistical_error_model` with
`reported_mass_error_model`, and `systematic_combined_with_statistical_error`
with `additional_systematic_scatter_added`. The historical admission field
`mbh_statistical_uncertainty_kind` remains for source-schema compatibility;
its values describe source-reported uncertainty semantics and do not imply
that calibration effects have been removed. Additional method envelopes stay
separate. This is a documented output-schema correction.

## Baccus publication sensitivity

The [published Table 1](https://content.cld.iop.org/journals/0004-637X/1006/2/165/revision1/apjae7de7t1_mrt.txt)
is saved and hashed independently from the frozen arXiv v1 table. Forty-four
frozen objects match by exact ID and within 0.5 arcsec. Five have no counterpart
within 0.5 arcsec; no ambiguous identity is forced. Of the 44 matches, 32 masses
change, with shifts from -0.10 to +0.04 dex.

`src/internal/assess_baccus_revision.py` substitutes published redshifts, masses,
quoted mass errors, luminosities, and Eddington ratios for matched objects.
Two scenarios retain or omit the five unmatched frozen objects, while keeping
evidence classifications and mass-method taxonomy fixed. No new sources are
admitted. The same deterministic 10,000-draw model is used for each scenario.
The two result tables are generated by the science notebook and independently
rebuilt by `verify_versions`:

- `results/v3/tables/v3_baccus_revision_comparison.csv`: all 49 objects, identity
  status, source values, shifts, and per-object growth/uncertainty diagnostics.
- `results/v3/tables/v3_baccus_revision_summary.csv`: the frozen baseline and
  both published-value scenarios.

All three scenarios retain the same top five by required Eddington ratio and
14 objects with point/median required f_Edd>1, 10 above unity at p16, 15 at p84,
and 8 with P(f_Edd>1)>=0.95. The unmatched-omitted scenario has 232 numerical
objects instead of 237. Individual lower-ranked values and ranks can change.

The headline conclusions are robust to these tested substitutions. Retain the
versioned frozen catalogue plus this sensitivity result for the current draft.
A future full publication re-admission would also review newly listed objects,
classification changes and selection; that is outside this explicitly bounded
measurement-version test. These counts are not demographic estimates.
